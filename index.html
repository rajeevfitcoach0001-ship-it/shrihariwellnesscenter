About us

A small centre in Kurukshetra, built on follow-through.

Shri Hari Wellness Center started from a simple frustration: most people who want to lose weight or get their sugar under control are handed a diet chart and left to manage alone. It rarely lasts more than a few weeks.

So we do it the other way round. You sit with us, we run a full body analysis, and we go through the report together. Then you get a plan built around your kitchen, your work hours and your family — plus a coach who checks in on you, rather than a chart you never hear about again.

Everything we use is herbal and nutrition-based. No medicine, no injections, no starving yourself. And if your case needs a doctor — sugar that is badly out of range, a thyroid that needs medication — we will say so plainly instead of selling you a package.
